Pollo air piano

Press Start to turn on the camera.

Drag to move. Drag an edge or corner to resize.

Play piano with your hands

  1. Prop your phone, tablet or laptop on the table with the front camera facing you.
  2. Hold your hands over the table so the camera sees your fingers.
  3. Tap Move keys to drag and resize the keyboard to where your hands are.

Video stays on your device. Hand tracking runs in the browser.
