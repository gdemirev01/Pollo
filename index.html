Play piano with your hands

  1. Prop your phone, tablet or laptop on the table with the front camera facing you. Or mount a phone above the table, back camera looking down, and pick Table mode in Settings.
  2. Hold your hands over the table so the camera sees your fingers.
  3. Hold your hands still where you want to play: the keyboard appears under your fingers. Tap Snap to hands to place it again. Use Move keys to fine-tune it, or drag its corners to match the table's angle.

Video stays on your device. Hand tracking runs in the browser.

Drag inside to move. Drag a corner onto the table to angle it. Drag an edge to resize.
